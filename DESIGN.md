## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, forward-looking aesthetic for an authorized electric vehicle dealership. Combining the technological confidence of pure electric mobility with the precision and reliability of modern automotive retail, the design expression lives at the intersection of **Corporate Modern** and **High-Tech Clean**. 

The digital space conveys immediate trust, frictionless booking, and aerodynamic elegance. Surfaces are luminous, clinical, and expansive, punctuated by the depth of automotive graphite, sharp electric blues, and vivid cyan accents reminiscent of illuminated LED vehicle headlamps and digital instrument clusters.

Visual principles:
- **Aerodynamic Clarity:** Generous whitespace, razor-sharp alignment, and uncluttered viewports that mirror clean automotive cockpit ergonomics.
- **Electric Precision:** High-contrast accents, purposeful glows, and crisp metallic-neutral framing that anchor performance metrics (acceleration, range, battery capacity).
- **Executive Trust:** Uncompromising legibility, straightforward pricing frameworks, and prominent, accessible conversion pathways (hotline assistance and test-drive reservations).

## Colors

The color palette centers on electric precision, anchored by deep brand blues and energetic luminescent highlights:

- **Primary (`#1464F4`):** Signature VinFast electric blue. Used for primary interactive triggers, primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, and high-impact navigational focal points.
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF`):** Energetic cyan. Reserved for high-tech accents, battery charging metrics, active EV mode tags, and glowing interactive hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#0B3B95`):** Deep automotive navy. Provides commanding contrast in hero headings, dark surface containers, footer blocks, and executive card backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937`):** Refined automotive dark gray. Anchors text hierarchy, subheadings, and baseline iconography.
- **Named Colors:**
  - `graphite-base` (`#111827`): Deepest graphite for high-contrast dark sections, cockpit-style visual panels, and fixed bottom utility trays.
  - `surface-subtle` (`#F4F6F9`): Clean metallic neutral floor wash used across page backgrounds to minimize glare while preserving crisp separation.
  - `surface-border` (`#E5E7EB`): Structural border tone for razor-thin card divisions and spec grids.
  - `dealership-red` (`#D80027`): VinFast heritage red accent, reserved strictly for urgent hotlines, limited-time inventory alerts, and promotional rebate badges.
  - `pure-white` (`#FFFFFF`): Pristine automotive showroom white for elevated cards, dialog surfaces, and input backgrounds.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on **Manrope**, leveraging its geometric architecture, open letterforms, and crisp tabular numbers to project technical proficiency.

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter spacing convey power and stance, mirroring vehicle presence on road. Mobile scales step down systematically to eliminate ungainly wrapping on compact screens.
- **Data & Metric Display (`stat-value`):** Dedicated numeric weight for fast scanning of range (km), battery sizes (kWh), acceleration (0-100 km/h), and base pricing.
- **Body & Captions:** Tuned for maximum legibility in technical specifications, warranty terms, and financing calculators.
- **Labels:** Uppercase styling paired with positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) is strictly utilized for technical badges, EV classification, and trim markers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid of **tonal surface stacking** and **subtle ambient shadows**, avoiding heavy skeuomorphism in favor of sleek, illuminated digital interfaces:

- **Level 0 (Showroom Ground):** `#F4F6F9` surface wash providing neutral baseline canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Deck):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px `#E5E7EB` border, resting on an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Showcase):** Elevated vehicle cards translate upward 2px with an expanded deep-tint ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 100, 244, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Utility & Hotlines):** Sticky bottom contact bars and floating booking triggers leverage backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` or `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.92)` and a top-edge highlight.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Configurators):** Centered popups backed by `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.6)` ambient scrim, with deep directional drop shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional **Soft (`1`)** shape language, reflecting precision engineering, sculpted sheet metal, and modern automotive dashboard interfaces.

- **Micro elements (Badges, Spec Chips, Inputs):** 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius, preserving athletic, sharp, technical structure.
- **Interactive Buttons & Selectors:** 6px to 8px (`0.375rem` to `0.5rem`) soft radius, balancing approachability with purposeful tactile feel. Full pills (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for live chat anchors and quick filter pills.
- **Showcase Cards & Media Panels:** 8px (`0.5rem`) outer radius with nested vehicle imagery cleanly clipped to identical interior radii.
- **Borders:** Crisp, razor-thin 1px outlines (`#E5E7EB`) define structural boundaries without visual heaviness.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (Test Drive / Deposit):** Solid `#1464F4` background, white text, 8px radius, bold Manrope label. On hover, smooth transition to `#0B3B95` with subtle cyan ambient glow.
- **Secondary Action (View Specs / Brochure):** White or `#F4F6F9` background with a 1.5px `#1464F4` stroke and `#1464F4` typography.
- **Hotline Urgent CTA:** Dealership Red (`#D80027`) solid fill with pulsing call icon indicator, white text, high urgency.

### Vehicle Showcase Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp white container with 1px border. Top compartment houses high-resolution vehicle render with floating trim badges (e.g., "VF 8 - Eco / Plus").
- **Spec Grid:** Integrated 3-column micro-matrix displaying Range (WLTP/NEDC), Max Power (hp), and Fast Charge Time (10-70%). Numeric values utilize `stat-value` styling.
- **Footer:** Transparent baseline strip displaying starting MSRP alongside dual CTAs: full specification link and a high-priority "Book Test Drive" button.

### Pricing Badges & Financial Tags
- **Base Style:** High-contrast compact badge using `#F4F6F9` background and `#0B3B95` text.
- **Promotion / Incentive:** Dealership Red tint background (`#FEF2F2`) with `#D80027` text and bold border tag ("Ưu đãi 100% Lệ Phí Trước Bạ").

### Sticky Dealership Hotline & Quick Action Bar
- Fixed bottom or floating dock housing direct sales phone line, Zalo chat gateway, showroom map location, and immediate test-drive trigger. Uses high-contrast graphite (`#111827`) with white text and an instant-call red action button.

### Form Inputs & Test Drive Selectors
- **Fields:** Single-line inputs with 4px border radius, `#F4F6F9` fill, and `#E5E7EB` outline. Focus state transitions border to `#1464F4` with a 3px outer ring at 15% opacity.
- **Showroom Location & Date Pickers:** Segmented chips allowing one-tap selection of model trims and preferred dealership visit times.

### Chips & Trim Selectors
- Pill or rounded-rect toggles (`0.25rem` radius). Unselected state utilizes neutral gray border; selected state receives `#1464F4` solid fill or high-contrast electric blue outline with active checkmark.
